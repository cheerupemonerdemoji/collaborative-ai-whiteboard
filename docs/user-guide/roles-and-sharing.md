# Roles and Sharing

Every person on a board has one role. The role decides what they can do on that board. The same person can have different roles on different boards.

## The roles

### Owner

Board administration plus editing. The person who creates a board is its Owner. An Owner can:

- Do everything an Editor can do.
- Invite people (the **Share** button) and choose their role.
- Change a member's role, or remove a member.
- Rename the board and delete it (a deleted board can be restored).

Each board has one Owner.

### Editor

Can work on the board's content and engineering records:

- Draw, edit, move, and delete things on the canvas.
- Create, edit, link, and archive records in the Semantic Inspector.
- Create and update Structured Evidence tables.
- Create checkpoints and restore earlier versions.

An Editor cannot invite people, change roles, rename, or delete the board.

### Viewer

Can look but cannot change anything:

- Can see the canvas, move around and zoom.
- Can browse the Semantic Inspector, read records and tables, and open History to look at earlier states.
- Cannot change the canvas, create or edit records, create checkpoints, or restore.

Viewers do not see the buttons for actions they cannot perform. Choose Viewer for stakeholders, reviewers, or anyone who only needs to read.

### Nonmember

Someone who is not on the board has no access at all. The board does not appear in their list and its link will not open for them.

## Inviting people

Only the board's Owner can invite people.

1. Open the board and click **Share**.
2. Under **Invite link**, choose the **Role** — **Editor** or **Viewer**.
3. Optionally, in **Invite a specific person**, type their username or email, so that only they can use the invitation.
4. Click **Create invite link**.
5. Click **Copy** and send the link to the person.

The link works for people who already have an account and for people who are new.

### Using an invitation

Opening an invitation link brings you to the sign-in page:

- **New to the whiteboard?** Choose **Create account**. The invitation is applied when you finish creating your account, and the board appears in your list.
- **Already have an account?** Choose **Sign in**. Once you are signed in you see **Join this whiteboard**; click **Accept invitation** and the board is added to your list.

If you were given an invitation code rather than a link, you can paste it into **Invitation code** when creating an account, or click **Join whiteboard** on **Your whiteboards** and paste it there.

### Good practice

- **Give the minimum role needed.** Start with Viewer; promote to Editor only when someone needs to change things.
- **Invitations are for one person.** Each invitation can be used once and expires after a while. Create a separate invitation for each person.
- **Name the person** when you can, so the invitation is not usable by someone else.

## Managing members

In the **Share** panel, **Members** lists everyone on the board. For each person except the Owner you can:

- Change their role between **Editor** and **Viewer** using the drop-down.
- Choose **Remove…** and then **Confirm** to remove them from the board.

If someone's screen looks out of date after you change their role, ask them to refresh the page.

## Your own boards

On **Your whiteboards**, each board card shows your role on it. Owners also see **Rename** and **Delete**. Deleting a board hides it; tick **Show deleted** to find it again and click **Restore**.
